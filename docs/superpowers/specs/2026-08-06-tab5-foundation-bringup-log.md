## Summary

8 of 9 items are fully PASS on real hardware. Item 9 (peripheral HAL detection) is now 2-of-3 confirmed (NFC, RFID2) with the third (RF433) meaningfully narrowed but not yet resolved — the physical connector is now confirmed shared with NFC/RFID2, but the specific data pin(s) within it remain unknown after a real-hardware test ruled out the most likely candidate (GPIO 53/54). Next step needs either a multimeter continuity check of the physical connector or better hardware documentation than what's publicly available (already exhausted). Item 5's functional flow is fully proven but carries a real, disclosed UX debt (QR/back-button) that should not be carried forward uncritically into future Tab5 UI phases.

No item is a FAIL. Phase 1's foundation — multi-target build, LVGL UI shell with working touch, dual WiFi/BLE C2 transport, PSK-based pairing, and the FeatureModule/FeatureRegistry contract proven end-to-end via a real feature — is complete and hardware-verified.
